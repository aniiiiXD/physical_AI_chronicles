The Milestone · π0.5

Cleaning a kitchen it has never seen

Physical Intelligence's π0.5 is, by the authors' claim, the first end-to-end learning system to perform long-horizon dexterous tasks — cleaning kitchens and bedrooms, making beds, loading sinks — in entirely new homes absent from training. The line between "lab demo" and open-world generalization.

SRC · pi.website/blog/pi05 · arXiv:2504.16054 · vote 3–0
The Architecture

Reason in language, then act

A hierarchical, chain-of-thought inference: the model first emits a high-level subtask in language, then conditions a 300M-parameter flow-matching action expert on it to produce continuous motor commands — co-trained on robot data, semantic prediction, and web data.

SRC · pi05.pdf §IV, App.E · vote 3–0
The Foundation · π0

The template the field copied

A flow-matching VLA on a PaliGemma backbone with action chunking, at up to 50 Hz. It folds laundry from a hamper, assembles boxes, busses tables, makes coffee, stacks eggs, bags groceries — tasks the authors say no prior system reached at that complexity.

SRC · arXiv:2410.24164 · vote 3–0
The Challenger · Gemini Robotics

Open-vocabulary dexterity

DeepMind's VLA on Gemini 2.0 outputs actions directly, generalizes to novel objects, positions and open-vocabulary instructions, and — fine-tuned — folds an origami fox and plays cards. >80% on half of 20 short-horizon dexterous tasks, beating SOTA baselines.

SRC · arXiv:2503.20020 · vote 3–0
Open Problems
  • Memory and coherence across very long task sequences remain brittle.
  • "Generalization" rests on small held-out sets (~3 homes vs. up to 104 in training); the systems are explicitly "far from perfect."
  • "Unseen environment" evaluations are still largely lab-adjacent, not true unstructured deployment.

Single Policy, Many Bodies

π0 as cross-embodiment generalist

One generalist trained on over 10,000 hours across 7–8 platforms — single-arm, dual-arm, mobile manipulators (UR5e, Franka, Trossen, Arx) — and 68 tasks. No per-platform retraining.

SRC · arXiv:2410.24164 · vote 3–0
The Shared Backbone

Open X-Embodiment

60 datasets from 34 labs pooled into 1M+ trajectories across 22 embodiments — arms to quadrupeds. The RT-X policy shows positive transfer: robots improve by leveraging other platforms' experience (RT-2-X ~3× on emergent tasks).

SRC · arXiv:2310.08864 · vote 3–0
Transfer to Humanoids

Gemini Robotics adapts

An ALOHA-2-pretrained model fine-tuned (not zero-shot) to a bi-arm Franka (~63% avg) and the Apptronik Apollo humanoid with five-fingered hands — adapting to completely novel embodiments via small-data fine-tuning.

SRC · arXiv:2503.20020 · vote 3–0
Grounded in Humans · HAT

Learning from human video

A unified 54-dim human-robot state-action space lets one Human Action Transformer train on human egocentric video (Vision Pro / Quest 3) plus robot data. Co-training yields ~100% per-task OOD gains and 20-shot transfer to a new humanoid.

SRC · arXiv:2503.13441 · vote 3–0
Open Problems
  • Cross-embodiment adaptation is mostly fine-tuning, not zero-shot transfer.
  • No predictive criterion exists for when positive transfer occurs — and it fails to appear on large, data-rich datasets without added model capacity.
  • Generalization to entirely new robot morphologies is largely unstudied.
03
Frontier Domain

On-Device Physical IntelligenceRunning capable models directly on the robot — the low-latency, real-time control physical tasks demand — without sacrificing capability, and acting even when connectivity is limited.

Indicative · Fetched, Not Formally Verified Leaders: Figure, DeepMind, NVIDIA
No claim in this domain survived the formal top-25 verification cut. The sources below are primary and fetched, but treat them as directional rather than adversarially confirmed.
Dual-System Control · Figure

Helix — fast hands, slow mind

A "System 1 / System 2" split pairing a slower VLM reasoner with a fast on-board reactive controller, the core pattern for resolving the tension between large backbones and tight real-time loops on a humanoid.

SRC · figure.ai/news/helix-02
On the Robot · DeepMind

Gemini Robotics On-Device

A VLA explicitly optimized to run locally on the robot with low latency and to operate under limited connectivity — bringing foundation-model capability off the cloud and onto the device.

SRC · deepmind.google/blog
The Substrate · NVIDIA

Jetson Thor

Positioned as the on-robot compute platform for physical AI — the hardware tier targeted by the current generation of on-device VLA deployments.

SRC · developer.nvidia.com/blog
Inference Technique · π

Real-Time Chunking

Physical Intelligence's method to make chunked VLA action generation viable in real time — directly attacking the latency cost of running large action models in the control loop.

SRC · pi.website/research/real_time_chunking
Open Problems
  • Quantitative latency / throughput results for capable VLAs on-robot under limited connectivity remain under-reported.
  • Model compression, distillation, and inference optimization for VLAs lack verified head-to-head benchmarks.
  • Which organization genuinely leads on-device capability-per-watt is not yet settled.

How this was made

A fan-out research harness.

Six search angles ran in parallel, 29 primary sources were fetched and dedup'd, 141 falsifiable claims extracted, and the top 25 were put through a three-vote adversarial check — a claim needed two of three votes to refute to be killed. Survivors were merged, ranked by confidence, and cited.

Caveat: source mix is dominated by first-party lab reports. Several headline capabilities are research demos, not independently audited deployments. π0 is late-2024; π0.5 and Gemini Robotics are spring 2025 — successors (π0.6, Gemini Robotics 1.5) supersede some numbers while the architectural claims hold.
